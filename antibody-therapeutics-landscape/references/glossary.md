# Glossary

Grouped by where the term shows up in a conversation, so neighbouring terms arrive together.

## Binding and biophysics

- **Affinity (KD)** — equilibrium dissociation constant, `kd/ka`; lower is tighter. Nanomolar is ordinary for a therapeutic, picomolar is tight.
- **ka / kon, kd / koff** — association and dissociation rate constants. Two antibodies with the same KD can behave differently in vivo: slow `koff` sustains target occupancy.
- **Avidity** — apparent strength gained from multivalent binding. A bivalent IgG on a densely expressed antigen looks far tighter than its monovalent affinity.
- **Epitope / paratope** — the patch on the antigen / the antibody surface that reads it. **Linear** epitopes survive denaturation; **conformational** ones do not.
- **Specificity vs cross-reactivity** — what it binds and what it also binds. Cross-reactivity to the orthologue in a tox species is a requirement, not a defect.
- **SPR, BLI** — surface plasmon resonance and biolayer interferometry; label-free kinetics. **KinExA** measures solution affinity when surface artefacts dominate.
- **Epitope binning** — competition sorting of a panel into epitope groups, so a lead panel covers distinct bins.
- **HDX-MS, alanine scanning, cryo-EM, X-ray crystallography** — epitope mapping at rising resolution and cost.

## Discovery

- **Hybridoma** — the classical route: immunise, fuse B cells with a myeloma line, clone.
- **Display** — phage, yeast or mammalian display of a library, selected by **panning** over rounds of enrichment.
- **Transgenic animal platforms** — mice carrying human immunoglobulin loci, giving human sequences straight from immunisation.
- **Single B-cell cloning** — sequencing paired heavy and light chains from sorted B cells of an immunised animal or a convalescent donor.
- **Affinity maturation** — directed improvement by mutagenesis and stringent re-selection.
- **Humanisation** — CDR grafting onto a human framework, or resurfacing; measured afterwards by germline identity and immunogenicity prediction.
- **Triage funnel** — thousands of hits narrowed by binding, function, epitope, species cross-reactivity and developability to a handful of leads and one development candidate.

## Developability

Properties that decide whether a good binder can become a product.

- **Aggregation** — monitored by SEC, DLS, and by thermal unfolding (**Tm** by DSF or nanoDSF, **Tagg**).
- **Viscosity** — matters when a subcutaneous dose needs >100 mg/mL in 1–2 mL.
- **Charge profile** — **pI** and charge variants resolved by CEX or cIEF. Extreme pI correlates with fast clearance and off-target tissue binding.
- **Hydrophobicity** — HIC retention; hydrophobic CDR patches predict aggregation and poly-specificity.
- **Polyreactivity** — PSR and baculovirus-particle ELISA screens for sticky antibodies.
- **PTMs** — glycosylation, deamidation, isomerisation, oxidation, C-terminal lysine clipping, N-terminal pyroglutamate. Sequence-level motifs are flags; see antibody-sequence-bioinformatics.
- **Forced degradation** — heat, light, low pH, freeze-thaw and agitation stress to expose instability early.

## PK, PD and effector function

- **FcRn** — the neonatal Fc receptor; pH-dependent recycling is why IgG lasts weeks.
- **TMDD** — target-mediated drug disposition: the target itself clears the drug, giving non-linear PK at low doses.
- **ADCC, ADCP, CDC** — cell-mediated cytotoxicity, phagocytosis, complement-dependent cytotoxicity.
- **Trogocytosis** — target stripped from the tumour cell surface, a resistance route for engagers and ADCs.
- **Receptor occupancy** — the PD readout linking exposure to effect.
- **Immunogenicity** — **ADA** (anti-drug antibodies), split into neutralising and non-neutralising; drives loss of exposure and infusion reactions.
- **Route** — IV versus subcutaneous, with bioavailability and dose-volume constraints.
- **Hook effect** — signal falling at high analyte concentration in a sandwich assay, a recurring artefact in bioanalysis.

## Clinical and regulatory

- **IND** (US) / **CTA** via **CTIS** (EU, Regulation 536/2014) — the filings that permit first dosing in humans.
- **Phase 1 / 2 / 3**, **dose escalation** (3+3, BOIN and other designs), **DLT**, **MTD**, **RP2D**.
- **Endpoints** — ORR, PFS, OS in oncology; disease-specific scores elsewhere. Note whether an endpoint is a surrogate.
- **Class adverse events** — CRS and ICANS for T-cell engagers, ILD and ocular toxicity for certain ADCs, infections for immunosuppressive antibodies.
- **BLA** (US, section 351(a)) / **MAA** (EU, centralised procedure through EMA's CHMP) — the marketing applications.
- **Designations** — orphan, breakthrough, fast track, accelerated approval (US), PRIME and conditional marketing authorisation (EU).
- **Label / SmPC** — the legal description of approved use; the authority on indication and safety.
- **Pharmacovigilance** — post-approval safety surveillance, with periodic reporting obligations.

## CMC and manufacturing

- **Cell line** — usually CHO; a **master** and **working cell bank** are banked and characterised.
- **Upstream / downstream** — fermentation (fed-batch or perfusion) to **titer**, then Protein A capture, polishing chromatography, viral clearance, UF/DF.
- **DS / DP** — drug substance and drug product; **fill-finish** sits between them.
- **CQA / CPP**, **QbD** — critical quality attributes and process parameters under quality-by-design.
- **Comparability** — the exercise proving a process change did not alter the product.
- **Tech transfer, scale-up, GMP, lot release** — the path from pilot to commercial supply, usually the real schedule driver.

## Commercial and access

- **Biosimilar** — a copy approved under an abbreviated route (US section 351(k)) on a comparability package rather than a full clinical programme; **interchangeability** is a separate US determination.
- **LOE / patent cliff** — loss of exclusivity and the revenue step-down that follows.
- **Licensing** — option fees, upfront, development and sales **milestones**, **royalties**, co-promotion.
- **CRO / CDMO** — outsourced research and outsourced manufacturing.
- **HTA and reimbursement** — the payer assessment after approval: NICE in England, G-BA/IQWiG in Germany, and in Norway the **Nye metoder** system with purchasing through Sykehusinnkjøp.
